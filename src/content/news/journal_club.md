Triton NeuroTech is hosting our **Week 7 Journal Club**, and it’s open to everyone across campus: whether you’re in neurotech, bioengineering, mechanical engineering, computer science, cognitive science, or just curious about the brain!

## We want you on stage to share anything neurotech-adjacent that excites you:

- Your own research, lab work, senior design, or side projects
- Recent news, breakthrough papers, or industry tech you’ve followed
- Cool concepts you want to talk about (BCIs, neuroprosthetics, neural decoding, biosignals, bio-robotics, etc.)

## Event Details:

- **Date**: Friday, November 13, 2026
- **Time**: 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM (Presentations start at 7:30 PM)
- **Location**: 2512 Henry Booker Room (Jacobs Hall, ECE Student Orgs)
- **Who**: Open to ANY UCSD student (undergrad, MS, PhD) from ANY major/club!

## How It Works:

- Present solo or with a team (one 5-minute slot per group).
- TNT officers will also be presenting what we’re working on, so come connect and see our research!
- No topic yet? [Sign up](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdsh-FNhFUDDy4aC6xBQEfT-ZRyWo-H3U9bxX7ezdkkVrd8Gw/viewform) anyway and we’ll help match you with an interesting paper.
- Keep it short, business casual, and fun—great low-stakes public speaking practice to add to your resume.
