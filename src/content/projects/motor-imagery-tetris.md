title: Motor imagery Tetris
year: "Earlier"
tracks: [EEG, ML]
summary: Tetris played with EEG. Players move pieces by imagining movements, using motor imagery signals from the brain.
---

When you imagine moving your left or right hand, the motor cortex on the opposite side of your brain changes its activity, even if you never actually move. This project used those motor imagery signals, recorded with EEG, to control Tetris pieces.
