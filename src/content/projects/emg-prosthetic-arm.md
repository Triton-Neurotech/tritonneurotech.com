Started in 2024–25 as one of our two Axon projects and continued in 2025–26. The team built a prosthetic arm that moves in response to muscle signals picked up by surface EMG electrodes.

The work covered:

- 3D printing and CAD for the arm itself
- Designing the EMG armband
- Arduino programming to drive the actuators
- Signal processing and machine learning to turn raw EMG into movement commands
